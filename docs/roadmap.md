# MOTIONIZE — Development Roadmap

### Strategic Development Trajectory

Motionize is envisioned as an evolving AI-powered visual creation platform. Its development follows a phased approach, prioritising foundational functionality, generative capabilities, operational reliability and progressive refinement.

## Phase 01 — Conceptualisation & Foundation
**Status: Initial development**

- Establishment of product vision and objectives.
- Identification of core creative functionalities.
- Preliminary interface and experience design.
- Selection of foundational technologies.
- Initial application architecture.

## Phase 02 — Core Application Development
**Status: In progress**

- Development of the primary application interface.
- Implementation of foundational application workflows.
- Integration of essential application components.
- Establishment of user interaction mechanisms.

## Phase 03 — Generative AI Integration
**Status: Planned**

- Integration of AI-powered image generation.
- Exploration of AI-driven video generation.
- Implementation of intelligent image manipulation.
- Integration of AI-assisted video editing capabilities.

## Phase 04 — Functional Expansion
**Status: Planned**

- Refinement of existing creative functionalities.
- Expansion of generative capabilities.
- Improvement of workflow continuity.
- Enhancement of the overall user experience.

## Phase 05 — Validation & Optimisation
**Status: Planned**

- Functional testing and validation.
- Performance optimisation.
- Reliability and compatibility assessment.
- Interface refinement and usability improvements.

## Phase 06 — Public Release
**Status: Future milestone**

- Final application refinement.
- Deployment preparation.
- Public accessibility.
- Continuous development and feature evolution.

---

*This roadmap represents the intended development direction. Milestones and timelines are subject to change as the project evolves.*
